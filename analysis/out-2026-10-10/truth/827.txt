With over 20 years of experience in the financial industry, Adham S. Kaibni spent the majority of his career in Wealth Management at Merrill Lynch in the D.C. Metro area. After 17 years at Merrill Lynch, he founded ASK Wealth Management powered by LPL Financial with the vision of offering clients a highly personalized boutique-level experience. Adham advises a variety of clientele, from high-net-worth individuals and corporate executives to corporations, non-profits, and business owners. He focuses on asset allocation, financial plans, and retirement planning to develop a comprehensive plan for his clients.

A native of the DC area, Adham received his degree in Finance from the University of Maryland. His hard work, combined with his honest and down-to-earth personality, is key to building strong relationships with his clients.

He currently resides in Coral Gables, Florida. Adham is a Chartered Retirement Planning Counselor (CRPC®) and holds his series 7, 31, and 66 regulations with LPL Financial.

CRPC conferred by College for Financial Planning.
