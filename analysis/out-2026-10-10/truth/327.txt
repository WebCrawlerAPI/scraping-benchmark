Activity tracking
Strava is great for tracking your workouts, but its built-in analytics can feel a bit limited if you're a data nerd (like me). That's why I built Statistics for Strava—a tool that digs deeper into your activity data to show trends, visualizations, and alternative stats that Strava doesn’t surface. Whether you're a weekend warrior or a hardcore athlete, you'll find new ways to explore your performance. You can run it on your own server, own your data, and even extend it if you like.
