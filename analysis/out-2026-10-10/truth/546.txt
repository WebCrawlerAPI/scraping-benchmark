Police
The Lethbridge Police Service (LPS) provides law enforcement and police services for community members in the City of Lethbridge.

If you are experiencing an emergency, please call 9-1-1 immediately. For any non-emergencies, contact the Lethbridge Police Service’s non-emergency number or email address.
