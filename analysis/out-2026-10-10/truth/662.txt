WARWICK, R.I., Sept. 10, 2024 — A new report from InsureMyTrip ranks the most popular international destinations by state. The data shows 31 states favor trips to Mexico, while other states prefer visiting Bahamas and Italy. Results are based on all travel insurance purchases for international trips departing in 2024.

Summary:

Mexico dominates the rankings as the top destination for about 60% of states.
Italy is a popular destination for 2024 – making the top 5 for every state except Oklahoma, Tennessee, West Virginia, and Wyoming.
Travel insurance customers from Hawaii pick Japan as a favorite destination.
Bahamas, The United Kingdom, and Canada all making strong showings in the rankings.

Travel Insurance for International Trips

According to InsureMyTrip, 87% of customers choose a Comprehensive Travel Insurance Policy for trips abroad. This policy provides a variety of benefits including medical coverage, emergency travel services, baggage protection, trip cancellation and trip interruption coverage.

Emergency medical coverage is especially important for international trips because most domestic health insurance plans do not cover medical bills outside of the country.
