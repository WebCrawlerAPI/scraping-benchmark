# Company Profile

**Offering world,&#x20;**&#x74;he finest floral concentrates and natural absolutes

Katyani Exports is a well established group engaged in manufacturing Natural Essential Oils, Spice Oils, Carrier Oils, Mint Oils and Floral Absolutes on a large scale. The company has two manufacturing units one is near orcha district in Madhya Pradesh for distillation of Essential Oils like Cypriol, Ajowain Oil, Cumin Oil, Dill Seed Oil, Carrot Seed Oil, Juniper Berry Oil, Sugandh Mantri Oil, Valerian Root Oil, etc. and other unit is in Mandsaur (Madhya Pradesh) for manufacturing of Herbal Extracts and Oleoresins.

## Our Products

We are manufacturing Natural Essential Oils, Spice Oils, Carrier Oils, Mint Oils and Floral Absolutes on a large scale. Our products are exported across the globe and we have firm presence in Europe, Asia, North America, Middle East etc. and continuing to expand through out world by serving products of International standards.
