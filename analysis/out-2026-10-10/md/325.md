# What is fat transfer breast augmentation?

**Fat transfer breast augmentation** essentially uses [liposuction](/cosmetic-procedures/liposuction "Liposuction") to take fat from other parts of your body and inject it into your breasts. This is a [breast augmentation](/cosmetic-procedures/breast-augmentation "Breast Augmentation") option for women who are looking for a relatively small increase in breast size and would prefer natural results.
