# Police

The [Lethbridge Police Service](https://www.lethbridgepolice.ca/) (LPS) provides law enforcement and police services for community members in the City of Lethbridge.

If you are experiencing an emergency, please call 9-1-1 immediately. For any non-emergencies, [contact the Lethbridge Police Service’s non-emergency number or email address](https://www.lethbridgepolice.ca/).

Visit their website to learn more about:

* Alarm system permits
* [Crisis support and victim services](https://www.lethbridgepolice.ca/victim-services)
* Filing a police report
* Fingerprinting
* Identity theft and fraud
* Pardon requests
* Recruitment and careers
* Requesting an information check
* Requesting a police file
