# Womenu0027s Try-a-Tri with Get Fit Guernsey

The Women’s Try-A-Tri with Get Fit Guernsey takes place in just over a fortnight, and all of us at OSA are proud to be sponsoring the swimming leg of the triathlon! Local women as young as 14 will be testing themselves in this fun and friendly event taking place at Pembroke and around the island’s north.

Our Director Sophie has been training hard for the events, swimming between La Vallette bathing pools and the Castle Cornet slipway as part of her preparation!

We’d like to wish all of the participants the very best of luck, particularly our Sophie!

For more information, see the **[Try-A-Tri Facebook event](https://www.facebook.com/events/849181838484118/)**.
