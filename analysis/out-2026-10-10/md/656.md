# Glens Falls – Hannaford Plaza

An 85,000 square foot shopping center located in Glens Falls, New York anchored by a Hannaford Supermarket. Inline tenants include Blockbuster Video, Hallmark, The UPS Store and Edward Jones. The outlot development is occupied by Bank of America.
