# Flat Roof Repair

## Emergency flat roof repairs after a disaster

Though extreme weather like tornadoes and hurricanes are rare in Quebec, storms with strong winds, heavy rainfall and lightning do occur and can damage flat roofs.

Disasters such as explosions and fire can also cause significant damage to flat roof buildings. All of which is to say: there are many accidents and unforeseen disasters that can leave you scrambling to repair your flat roof.

Our **emergency roof repair service** can quickly seal and protect your flat roof from [leaks and water damage](https://www.lescouvreursdurotoit.com/en/water-infiltration-through-a-flat-roof/). You can count on our professional team for fast and effective solutions.

## A fast response to repair your flat roof

### Flat roof inspection

Our roofers can conduct an emergency [flat roof inspection](https://www.lescouvreursdurotoit.com/en/flat-roof-inspection/) to accurately assess the damage. After this consultation, you will know exactly what steps to take and what materials are needed to repair your roof after a disaster. You will also have a good idea how much the repairs will cost.

If you need to file a claim for the damage, we will also support you through the claims process with your insurance company.

### Installation of temporary waterproof covers

Before beginning the flat roof repair, we can immediately install temporary waterproof covers to prevent further water infiltration and damage.

### Complete repair of your damaged flat roof

Flat roofs are very different from pitched roofs. Depending on the damage done, flat roof repair after an emergency can entail:

* [Repairing the flat roof structure](https://www.lescouvreursdurotoit.com/en/flat-roof-structure/)
* [Replacing the ventilation system](https://www.lescouvreursdurotoit.com/en/flat-roof-ventilation/)
* [Replacing the waterproof membrane](https://www.lescouvreursdurotoit.com/en/flat-roof-membrane-system/) (see also: [membrane type](https://www.lescouvreursdurotoit.com/en/flat-roof-membrane/))
* [Replacing damaged insulation](https://www.lescouvreursdurotoit.com/en/flat-roof-insulation/)
* And more.
