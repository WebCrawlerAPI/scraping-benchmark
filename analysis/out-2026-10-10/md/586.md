# T-type Hinges

## Soft-Close Hinges

Titus soft-close hinges are designed to eliminate slamming and enhance user experience through controlled, silent closing. Featuring integrated damping systems, these hinges automatically slow door movement for a smooth finish. Manufactured to meet international performance standards, they provide durability, reliability, and premium functionality for kitchens, wardrobes, and furniture applications.

**What is a T-type hinge?** A T-type hinge is a concealed cabinet hinge designed for fast installation and precise alignment.\
**Are concealed hinges durable?** Yes, they are cycle-tested for long-term performance and include a lifetime warranty, plus CATAS and FIRA Level H certifications.\
**Are T-type hinges soft-close?** Yes, Titus T-type Glissando hinges include integrated soft-close damping.
